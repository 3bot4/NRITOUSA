## Subtopics nobody covers well — our unique sections

1. **The combined L-and-H clock.** The single most expensive misunderstanding on this route for an Indian worker, and it is one sentence of (l)(12). We give it a calculator.
2. **The $4,500 fee, now on every renewal.** The 50/50 employer definition describes most large Indian IT services firms exactly. Before 9 September 2026 an extension escaped the fee; now it doesn't.
3. **Blanket L is not a shortcut for everyone at your employer.** Professionals only; consular denial is final; and the reassignment rule in (l)(5)(ii)(G) — you may be moved between listed entities without re-filing only if the duties are *virtually the same*.
4. **Off-site placement under the L-1 Visa Reform Act**, written for the worker rather than the employer: what your petition has to assert about who supervises you, and why a client-site L-1B is the most RFE-prone filing on this route.
5. **L-2S, and the money it is worth.** An L-2 spouse works without an EAD; an H-4 spouse generally cannot. For a two-income Indian family choosing between an H-1B transfer and an L-1 transfer, that is the largest single financial difference between the two routes, and it is invisible in every L-1-vs-H-1B table we read.
6. **The India admissions picture:** 10.8% of L-1 admissions against 55.9% of H-1B — L-1 is the second-largest Indian work route and still an order of magnitude smaller than H-1B.
7. **Interactive: L-1 eligibility and max-stay calculator.** Months worked abroad in the last 3 years, role type, months already spent in L *and* H status → eligibility flags plus remaining stay against the right cap. Browser-only. No competitor has one.
